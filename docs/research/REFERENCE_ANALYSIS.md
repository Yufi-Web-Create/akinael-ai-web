## Synthesis
Hero: plain Japanese audience + concrete production scope immediately; brand line stays central.
Navigation: 4 essential destinations, a separate customer login, explicit mobile menu.
CTA: action + expectation; free consultation goes to an onboarding explanation, never masquerades as a logged-in chat.
Typography: strong title/body hierarchy; Japanese body 16px minimum; restrained English.
Layout: broad editorial sections and ruled lists; no repeated three-card rows.
Imagery: existing brand mark only; typography is intentionally sufficient, permitted by owner brief. No invented customer images.
Pricing/FAQ: fee event and excluded scope before commitment. Details disclosure is native HTML.
Mobile: title broken into meaningful phrases; CTA in natural flow; stacked content, no horizontal carousels.
Distinctive approach: business owner's request in everyday language paired with the deliverable and the approval boundary.
